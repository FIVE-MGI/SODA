1. Prepare a folder of datasets

Put each dataset in its own folder inside one data folder (a Visium HD outs folder with its image, a Xenium output folder, a CosMx or MERSCOPE folder, a GeoMx folder, or one OME-TIFF / QPTIFF / .h5ad file, a zipped Xenium output or a Xenium *_transcripts.zarr.zip; names starting with _ are left out). SODA works out what each one is, prepares and exports it, then checks the export for sample IDs. Your data folder is only read, never changed.
